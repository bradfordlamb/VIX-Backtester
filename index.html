⚠ Important Disclaimer

This tool is for educational and analytical purposes only.

VIX options are priced from VIX futures, not spot VIX. Any option pricing estimate based solely on spot VIX is an approximation and may differ materially from actual historical option prices. Actual option premiums depend on the VIX futures term structure, which can trade at a significant premium or discount to spot VIX.

The Black-Scholes model used here does not perfectly model VIX options due to the mean-reverting nature of volatility, jump risk, and the futures-based settlement mechanism.

Past performance of any strategy does not guarantee future results. Options trading involves substantial risk of loss and may not be suitable for all investors. This is not financial advice.

For real trade decisions, use actual historical option chain data from CBOE or a data provider.
